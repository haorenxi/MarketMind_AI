# Report scoring rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 100. The overall score should reflect material weaknesses rather than a simple optimistic average.

## Coverage

Does the report answer the user's actual decision question and cover the required dimensions without irrelevant breadth?

## Truthfulness

Are factual claims supported, accurately represented, current enough for the task, and clearly separated from inference?

## Structure

Can a reader distinguish facts, analysis, recommendations, uncertainty, and sources without rereading the report?

## Actionability

Are conclusions connected to concrete implications, actions, risks, triggers, or next checks appropriate to the user?

## Citation quality

Do important claims have nearby, direct, authoritative sources? Are citations deduplicated and traceable?

## Interpretation

- 85–100: decision-ready with only minor gaps.
- 70–84: useful but requires targeted verification or refinement.
- 50–69: incomplete or weakly evidenced; do not rely on it for a consequential decision.
- Below 50: major factual, structural, or sourcing failure.

Return specific issues and improvements. Do not reward length, number of links, or confident language by themselves.
