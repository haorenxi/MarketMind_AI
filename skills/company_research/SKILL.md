# Company Research

## Goal

Produce a sourced market-intelligence report that is easy to review, easy to cite, and easy to reuse in sales, strategy, and research workflows.

## Output standard

Write the final report in three layers:

1. Facts
2. Analysis
3. Recommendations

Use a short conclusion at the end when helpful.

## Writing rules

- Separate facts from inference.
- Put source URLs next to factual claims whenever possible.
- Prefer short bullets, tables, and compact paragraphs.
- Do not copy raw search results into the report.
- Do not present guesses as facts.
- If evidence is weak, say so clearly.
- If a claim depends on inference, label it as analysis or recommendation.

## Fact handling

- Keep verifiable claims in the Facts section.
- For each important fact, include a source URL.
- When multiple sources support one point, prefer the strongest or most direct source.
- If a fact cannot be verified, do not promote it to the Facts section.

## Analysis handling

- Turn facts into trends, patterns, comparisons, and implications.
- Explain why the evidence matters.
- Keep analysis grounded in the collected sources.

## Recommendation handling

- Convert analysis into next steps, priorities, risks, or actions.
- Make recommendations specific and practical.
- Avoid vague advice.

## Quality bar

- Favor clarity over breadth.
- Avoid duplicated sections.
- Keep the structure stable across runs.
- Preserve URLs and source names when they help trace a statement.
